---
title: Assignment updates
description: When the teacher publishes a fix, students bring it in with a guided merge, not git.
---

Assignments have typos and broken tests, and teachers fix them after students
have started. With Yukibana, the teacher publishes a new [release](/docs/cloud/releases)
and the IDE offers every student to bring their folder up to date, without
anyone needing to know git.

## How the student sees it

When a newer release exists, Yukibana announces it with a notification, an item
in the status bar, and an **Assignment Update** view in the side panel. "Later"
never loses it: the status bar item and the view stay until the update is
applied. The submit dialog also says when a newer version is waiting.

The IDE checks every five minutes, and every minute in the two hours before the
deadline.

## What the update does

In one step, the update writes every file that needs no decision:

- **read-only files**, which always take the teacher's version;
- **files only the teacher changed**.

Files **both** the student and the teacher changed keep the student's version
and are listed to settle: in Theia's three-way merge editor, or with **Keep
mine** or **Use teacher's**. A student's own edits to a read-only file are
always replaced, but as an explicit step, with **Review my changes** offered
first.

Every file the update touches is backed up first. One **Undo** puts it all
back, and an update interrupted halfway is rolled back the next time the IDE
checks.

<Callout title="Why not git?">
  Many students are not yet fluent in git, and a merge conflict is often met by
  accepting everything. The guided update keeps the decisions few and explicit,
  and only asks about files where both sides really changed.
</Callout>

## On the teacher's side

Each submission records the release it started from. Staff see which
submissions are not based on the newest release, and the CLI warns about them
when downloading. Assembly measures a student's changes against the release
they started from, so a teacher's later fix is never reported as the student's
change. See [Submissions and grading](/docs/cloud/submissions#assembly).
