<Callout type="warn" title="Early groundwork">
  Telemetry is in an early state and is not sent anywhere by default. This page
  describes what exists and where it is headed.
</Callout>

Yukibana has a telemetry extension that collects events reported under
`yukibana/` topics inside the IDE (opening a project, submitting, …), batches
them, and sends them to a telemetry server. A small companion server receives
the reports.

## Why

Most of the questions teachers have about programming exercises are about the
*process*, not the final file: where students get stuck, how much of the code
was typed, pasted or generated, and whether an exam was taken under the
conditions it was meant for. Telemetry is the basis for answering them, for
example:

- detecting large pastes and tracking use of completions and AI assistance;
- replaying how a solution was written, and a live view for invigilators;
- spotting students who are stuck;
- helping detect cheating in [exam settings](/docs/vision#exams-and-proctoring).

Any of this will be opt-in per course and designed with students' privacy in
mind. See the [roadmap](/docs/vision).
