---
title: For students
description: Open an exercise, work on it in Yukibana, and hand it in.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install Yukibana and log in to the cloud

[Install the IDE](/docs/getting-started/install), then go to
[cloud.yukibana.dev](https://cloud.yukibana.dev) and log in with GitHub, or with a
one-time code sent to your email address. There is no account to create: your
teacher enrolled your address, and your courses are there at your first login.

</Step>
<Step>

### Find your exercise

The **To do** tab lists what is still to hand in across all your courses,
soonest deadline first, and what you have already handed in. An exercise
appears once it opens and disappears when it closes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open it in Yukibana

Click **Open in IDE**. Your browser hands a `yukibana://project/open?id=…` link
to Yukibana, which asks where to put the project, downloads the starter into
that folder, and opens it in this window or a new one.

The workspace is set up the way your teacher configured it: the assignment opens
in its own panel, the files you need are open, and only the tools meant for this
exercise are shown. Some files, typically the tests, are read-only.

</Step>
<Step>

### Work, run, test

Write your code, then run it and run the tests.

</Step>
<Step>

### Submit

Click **Submit**. Yukibana logs you in to the cloud if needed (through your
browser), shows the exact list of files that will be sent, and sends them once
you confirm. You can submit as many times as you like: every version is kept,
and the newest is the one that counts.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Deadlines and late work

An exercise has a window: it becomes visible on its opening date and stops
accepting work on its closing date, if it has one. The **deadline** sits inside
that window. After it you can still submit, and your submission is recorded as
**late**. If your teacher extends the deadline, work you already handed in
counts as on time.

## Without the IDE

Everything also works from a terminal with the [`yukibana` CLI](/docs/reference/cli),
and from the cloud's web pages, where you can download the starter and upload an
archive.

```bash
yukibana login --url https://cloud.yukibana.dev
yukibana projects --open        # what you can hand in now
yukibana starter <project-id>   # download and unpack the starter
cd <project>
yukibana submit                 # lists what would be sent, asks, sends
```
